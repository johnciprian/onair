# Changelog

Each version's section becomes its release notes: on the GitHub release and in OnAir's update window.

## 1.1

- **About OnAir** in the menu shows the version, a link to this project, and the license.
- **Show On Screen** items are now switches, and the menu stays open while you flip them.
- Icons on About, Check for Updates, Settings, Quit, and Mute, with Settings in its own section.

## 1.0

The first release.

- A menu bar sign that shows whether your Zoom microphone is live (**ON AIR**) or muted (**OFF AIR**).
- One hotkey: tap to mute or unmute, hold while muted to talk, hold while live to mute for a moment.
- Optional on-screen cues: a floating badge, a pulsing red glow around the screen edges, and a flash in the
  middle of the screen on every mute change.
- Instant feedback, even though Zoom takes up to about a second to confirm a change.
- Automatic updates.
